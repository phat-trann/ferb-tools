# Version history

Current version: `1.20.1`. Related patch releases are grouped below.

## 1.20.0–1.20.1

- Adds Light, Dark, and System appearance with a consistent blue accent.
- Adds the SFCC Toolkit URL fix with a saved on/off switch.
- Shares Jira site and Role settings across KPI and reports.
- Uses Completed Date for On Deadline and shows N/A when date data is unavailable.
- Improves security, export performance, and reminder reliability.
