Features

Tools for your daily work.

Check-in Reminder

Save your check-in time and get a reminder when it is time to check out. Choose your work hours and rounding.

Content Asset Export

Export one content asset as XML from its SFCC detail page, including translations and site-specific data.

Content Asset List Export

Paste content IDs on the SFCC search page and export them together into one XML file.

Monthly KPI

Track work time, deadlines, bug fixing, production issues, and NCs using Jira and 1Office data for your selected month.

Jira Work Plan

Create an editable plan with overdue tasks, upcoming deadlines, and estimates. Copy it when ready.

Jira Result

Choose a date, generate your completed-task report, then edit and copy it.

Fix SFCC Toolkit URL

Keep Storefront Editing links pointing to the right STG or DEV host, with a simple on/off switch.

Make it yours

Choose Light, Dark, or System appearance. Hide unused tools and manage your settings from the gear icon.

Install

Ready in three steps.

1

Download and unzip

Get ferbtools-production.zip from Releases and extract it.

2

Enable Developer mode

Open chrome://extensions in Chrome and turn on Developer mode.

3

Load unpacked

Select Load unpacked, choose the extracted folder, and open FerbTools from the toolbar.
